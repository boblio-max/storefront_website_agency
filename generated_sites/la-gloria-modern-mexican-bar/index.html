Our Story

A modern cantina in the middle of Canyon Park

La Gloria Modern Mexican Bar is the evenings-up version of your favorite taqueria: same family recipes, longer nights. At 22010 17th Ave SE in Bothell, we run a wood-fired grill that does carne asada right, a kitchen that presses corn tortillas before service, and a bar that treats agave as seriously as the neighborhood treats Sundays.

The dining room is low light and warm — ember tones, papel picado overhead, a long bar where mezcal flights get poured under $20. It is the kind of place where a Tuesday happy hour becomes a Thursday taco night becomes a loud Friday DJ set.

  • Nixtamalized corn, pressed in-house every day
  • Happy hour 4–6 daily with $8 tacos and $7 margs
  • Private patio & back room for parties up to 60
  • Live DJ Fridays and weekend brunch from noon
