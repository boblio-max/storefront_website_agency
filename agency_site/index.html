Fair questions

The stuff everyone asks before saying hello.

Why is the preview free?

Because it's the best way to earn your trust. It takes me a couple of days to design — most owners approve it, and that's my whole sales pitch. If you don't love it, keep the ideas, no hard feelings.

How long does it take?

Preview in about 48 hours. After you approve, launch is usually same-day — I connect your domain myself.

Do I own the website?

Completely — domain, design, words, everything. I'll hand over every file whenever you ask. No lock-in.

I'm not techy at all. Is that okay?

That's most of my clients. You never touch code, dashboards, or DNS settings — you email me like you'd text a neighbor, and I handle the rest.

What do you need from me to start?

The form below — your name and what your business does is plenty. I'll look up the rest and confirm everything with you before launch.
