06 — Fair questions

Asked, answered

Why free? What's the catch?

Two days of my time, and most owners approve the preview — that's the entire sales pitch. If you don't love it, keep the ideas. Genuinely no hard feelings; it happens.

Are you actually one person?

Yes. That's the point — you talk to the person drawing your site, not an account manager relaying notes to a contractor.

Do I own everything?

Domain, design, words, files. I'll zip it all up whenever you ask, even if you're leaving. No lock-in is a selling point, not a risk.

I'm hopeless with computers.

Perfect, most clients are. You'll never see code, a dashboard, or a DNS record. Email me like a neighbor and consider it done.

What do you need from me?

The form below: name, business, email. I'll look up the rest and confirm every fact with you before launch.
