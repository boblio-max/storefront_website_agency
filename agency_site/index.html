07 — Fair questions

Asked, answered

Why free? What's the catch?

There isn't one. Designing your preview is how I show my work instead of describing it — if you don't love it, keep the ideas and we part friends.

Will I work with a real person?

Yes — me. You talk to the person drawing your site, and every reply comes from a human.

Do I own everything?

Domain, design, words, files. I'll zip it all up whenever you ask, even if you're leaving. No lock-in is a selling point, not a risk.

I'm not technical. Is that okay?

You wouldn't be the first. You'll never see code, a dashboard, or a DNS record. Email me like a neighbor and consider it done.

What do you need from me?

The form below: name, business, email. I'll look up the rest and confirm every fact with you before launch.
